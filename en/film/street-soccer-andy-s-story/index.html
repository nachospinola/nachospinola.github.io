Behind every player there is a story you can't see from the stands. Andy's starts in Glasgow, goes through a football pitch and ends up much further than the scoreboard shows.

Documentary video about Andy, player of the Scottish male's team of Street Soccer. Street Soccer Scotland uses football as a social tool, and this portrait follows Andy to understand what being part of the team means to him: the training, the teammates and everything that happens off the pitch once the match is over.
