Street SoccerStories

Behind every player there is a story you can't always see from the stands. Andy's, Tyler's and Carol's are stories of difficult moments, of overcoming and of starting over. Three very different lives that found in football something more than a sport.

Three documentary portraits for Street Soccer Scotland, an organization that uses football as a tool for social inclusion. Through their testimonies, we get to know their personal stories, the hardest moments they have been through and how they have found in sport and in their teammates a way to keep going. My job was to approach them with trust, listen without judging and let them tell their stories in their own words.
