Ashoka Jóvenes Changemakers

Some young people don't wait to grow up before changing things. This is the story of Amaya's project, one of Ashoka's 2021 Changemakers, and of the energy with which she stands up for it.

Video made about the project of Amaya, Ashoka's young changemaker 2021. A portrait of what it means to start something small, with more enthusiasm than resources, and watch it grow. A piece for Ashoka that aims to inspire other young people to take the first step and to believe that their idea can matter too.
