Street SoccerIndia

A football needs no translation. Not in Glasgow, not in Nagpur. Football is a universal language able to break down barriers, bring communities together and transform lives.

Street Soccer, a Scottish organization that uses football to support people in vulnerable situations, invited me to join them on the trip and make a film that authentically reflected these women's experience. To tell their story from the inside, I had to become one of the team, sharing every moment of the experience: travelling, enjoying and living alongside them from the first day to the last.
