SRNAThe power of community

Six days. Six cities. Six stories. Behind every rare disease is a journey of challenges, resilience, and hope.

Through a series of six documentary portraits, we explored SRNA’s 30 years of work in the United States and its impact on people living with rare diseases. With just one week to travel across the country, every detail of the production had to be carefully planned. Beyond the logistical challenge, our priority was to give each person the time, attention, and space they deserved, capturing their experiences with honesty, sensitivity, and care.
