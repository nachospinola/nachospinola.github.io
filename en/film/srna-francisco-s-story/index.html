SRNA

When an illness is so rare that almost nobody knows it, telling it is the first step for someone to recognise it. This is the story of Francisco and the family that stands by him every day.

Video created for SRNA (Siegel Rare Neuroimmune Association), telling Francisco’s story. A piece made with patience and warmth, meant to help other families living something similar feel accompanied, and to help those who don't know these illnesses understand why it matters to research them and talk about them.
