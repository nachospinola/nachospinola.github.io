Plena InclusiónHistorias de amistad

Friendship doesn't understand labels. This project puts images to a book that celebrates what it means to have someone by your side, with or without an intellectual disability.

A project made for Plena Inclusión, in collaboration with A Toda Vela, to bring images to the making of the book “Friendship Stories”, written by Isabel Guirao, which values the role of friendship for people with intellectual disabilities. Everyday scenes, walks and shared laughter that go alongside the stories in the book. A trailer to invite people to read it and, above all, to look at friendship between people with different eyes.
