Wings of Heart

A place where rescued animals can finally live in peace, and where the people who look after them make it look easy. A sanctuary told from the inside.

Video made for the Animal Sanctuary Wings of Heart. A walk through the sanctuary's daily life and through the quiet, constant bond between those who care and those who are cared for. A film to share its work and to encourage viewers to come and see it for themselves.
