Homeless World CupEqual Game

A day to remember that on the pitch we all play by the same rules. Cardiff, a world tournament and a message of respect that fits inside a football.

Video made for the Respect Day UEFA during the Homeless World Cup. A short, direct piece that captures the atmosphere of the tournament and the idea that drives it: that sport can be a place where nobody is left out and where everyone, wherever they come from, has something to bring to the team.
