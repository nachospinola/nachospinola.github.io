Homeless World CupEqual Game

When the ball starts rolling, the differences disappear. The Homeless World Cup is a global movement that uses football as a tool for social change and brings together people affected by homelessness and exclusion under one shared goal: to play.

Each year, through Equal Game, an initiative developed in collaboration with UEFA, I took on the challenge of creating a short film through the language of football, capturing the power of sport to bring together people from different backgrounds under one shared goal: to play. Beyond differences of religion, competitiveness, culture or gender, the aim was to distil the full spirit of the Homeless World Cup into a single piece.
