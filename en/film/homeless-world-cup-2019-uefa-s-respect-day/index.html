Homeless World CupEqual Game

When the ball starts rolling, the differences disappear. Because sometimes, football is so much more than a game.

The Homeless World Cup is a global movement that uses football as a tool for social change. Each year, through Equal Game, an initiative developed in collaboration with UEFA, I took on the challenge of creating a spot capable of capturing the spirit of the World Cup and the power of sport to bring together people of different backgrounds, cultures, religions and genders. A piece that, through the language of football, would show everything that unites us beyond our differences.
