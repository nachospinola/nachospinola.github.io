Homeless World CupStories

Sometimes a single story explains why a whole tournament exists. This is Dulce's, who crossed the ocean to play in Oslo and talks about football as something more than a sport.

Documentary short film about Dulce, Mexico’s player at the Homeless World Cup. An intimate, unhurried portrait of a player, of what football has changed in her life and of what it means to represent her country in a World Cup that isn't played for trophies but for second chances.
