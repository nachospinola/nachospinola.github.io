CepaimPor un mundo nuevo

Some people aren't asking for a better world but for a different one: a place where nobody has to justify where they come from in order to stay.

For so many people a better world is just not enough. They need a new world, where who you are is more important than where you are from. A film made for CEPAIM about migration and living together, meant more to start a conversation than to give an answer. Faces, glances and silences to remind us that behind every migrant story there is a person.
