Compasión Animal

Caring for an animal that has suffered is also a way of believing again. The daily life of a sanctuary, told without hurry and with a lot of affection.

Video made for the Animal Sanctuary "Compasion Animal". A film to share the sanctuary's daily work, the faces of the people who make it possible and the calm of the animals that find a home there. An invitation to look closely and, perhaps, to join in.
