I used to want to change the world with my films. Now I also want to show mine.

Since 2015 I've made documentary films for social organizations, both in Spain and internationally. More than ten years listening to stories that deserved to be told, getting to know other realities and trying to show them honestly.

Now that I'm a father, the way I look at things has changed. I've discovered a world that is small and enormous at once: homes, games, the afternoon light and the endless noise of children. I've realised you don't have to go very far to find moments we would always want to remember, and photography has become another way of keeping them.

Today, these two worlds live together more than ever. Film has taught me to listen, to observe and to leave space for things to happen. Family photography lets me do the same from a closer place, where the everyday becomes the protagonist.

In the end, maybe they were never such different things. Because big stories and small ones alike deserve someone who stops to look at them.
