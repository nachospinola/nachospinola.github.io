I used to want to change the world with my films. Now I want to show mine.

I'm Nacho. Since 2015 I've made documentary films for social organizations, in Spain and abroad. More than ten years listening to stories that deserved to be told, hoping that telling them could change something.

Since becoming a father, the way I look has changed. There's a world that is small and enormous at once: homes, games, the afternoon light and children who never sit still. Now I want to show it through photography.

The two worlds live side by side. The one who makes films still believes a well-told story can move something. The one who photographs families believes real life, without posing, is already beautiful enough. Each one feeds the other.

If you have a cause to tell, or a family you want to keep just as it is, write to me.
